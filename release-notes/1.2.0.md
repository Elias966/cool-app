## What's new in 1.2.0

**Prism for Android**
- Prism now comes as an Android app (`Prism-1.2.0.apk`) for phones and tablets running Android 7.0 or newer, with all eight modules.
- Every page is laid out for touch screens: a bottom tab bar and one-column modules on phones, a slim side rail in landscape, the full layout on tablets, finger-sized buttons, and the keyboard only opens when you tap a text box.
- Sound re-tuned for phone speakers (deep tones stay audible, crisper effects) plus haptic taps on presses, stamps, errors and successes. Vibration and typing clicks can be switched in the speaker menu.
- The local AI runs on the phone too, with a smaller model (Qwen2.5-0.5B, about 520 MB, downloaded the first time you open an AI mode, then offline).
- Android back button/gesture goes back to Home; copying uses the Android clipboard and Cipher Pact key cards open the share sheet.

**Linux**
- The AppImage is built from the same code and works as before.
