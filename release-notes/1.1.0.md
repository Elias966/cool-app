## What's new in 1.1.0

**Three new modules**
- **Layered Encoding**: stack Base64, ROT13, Reverse and 10 other encoders on the same string, then peel them back off (auto-detects the layers).
- **Japanese Scripts**: katakana loanwords, hiragana and halfwidth ciphers, romaji mix and look-alike kanji, with furigana and vertical writing; reads any kana back as romaji.
- **Cipher Pact**: a private cipher with a friend. Every message gets a brand-new random alphabet, the local AI writes a lesson note, and you seal your secret in it. Five message styles, Read and Practice modes, key cards.

**Sound**
- Synthesized sound effects everywhere: navigation, clicks, typing, and every animation, plus a custom intro and signature sounds for each module. Volume, mute and typing clicks are in the title bar.

**Also**
- A Clear history button in every module.
