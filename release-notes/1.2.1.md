## What's new in 1.2.1

**Decoding gives back exactly what you wrote**
- Every module now round-trips: paste a result into its decode mode and you get the original text back, letter for letter, with capitals, accents, punctuation and line breaks.
- Japanese Scripts: katakana and Kanji-look results used to come back as approximate romaji ("from" read as "Furomu", capitals lost). Results now carry an invisible copy of the original, and katakana from elsewhere is read with a 64,000-word English list (フロム → from).
- Ancient Scripts: runes, hieroglyphs, Ogham, cuneiform and Greek no longer misspell words ("kuikk" → quick, "wery" → very), and the Greek question mark reads back as "?".
- Cipher Pact: capitals and accents survive sealing; the Word swap style handles words with apostrophes and words that differ only in case; Number map handles hyphens; multi-line secrets come back whole. The secret itself is never stored in the message.
- Braille (tabs, accents), Dingbats (symbols shared between fonts), Layered Encoding (Auto-peel now undoes chains made in Prism exactly) and Malbolge (non-ASCII text) also come back exactly.
- Fixed: typing "constructor" in Katakana mode broke the conversion.

**Android**
- Vibration works again: it no longer depends on the phone's own "touch feedback" setting (use the Vibration switch in the speaker menu).
- Typing clicks now play with the on-screen keyboard, and are on by default.
